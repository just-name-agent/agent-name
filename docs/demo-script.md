# Demo script — 3 minutes (seed 2, mock mode, accelerated replay)

Record at 1080p. Show the banner in every shot. Narrate what the engine
decides, not what the models say.

- 0:00 Battery hook. Day slider on 1. "Six robots. 32 charge each.
  Seven days. No stock, no tools, one ruined workshop."
- 0:20 Create the 6th robot. Show voice transcribe (or the typed
  backstory in mock mode). NOVA appears in the circle.
- 0:50 Day 1 intros. Timeline shows 6 DISCUSS lines. "Talk only. No vote."
- 1:20 First vote fight. Scrub to day 2-4. Unanimous scrap, then the
  day-4 tie: grant NONE. "The engine gave them nothing."
- 1:50 Engine rejects on camera. Day-4 fails: three robots raced one
  kit, engine lists exact shortage. "Talk cannot create steel."
- 2:20 Built power. Day 6 second trickle, day 7 HALO builds the GRID.
  Panels and tower appear in 3D. Metrics update.
- 2:50 Honest ending + future. "Seed 2 survives. Seed 3 browns out —
  same rules." Show sample_run_brownout.json metrics. Roadmap card:
  persistent city, more recipes, SDK, coordination benchmark.

Fallbacks: if the venue has no internet, the Three.js CDN fails — record
the video at home and play the file. If the API key dies mid-demo, the
pause banner appears; narrate the quota rule and switch to the recording.
